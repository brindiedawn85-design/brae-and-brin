Travel & Stay

Flying In

Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC) is the closest major airport, about an hour’s drive from Sundance. Rental cars and rideshares are available at the airport.

Where to Stay

Cabins and rooms at Sundance Mountain Resort are limited and book early. Provo, Orem, and Park City also have plenty of options. A guest room block may be shared with your invitation.

Getting Around

Mountain roads can be winding, and weather can change quickly. Leave extra time on the day, and check road conditions before you travel.

Questions

Good to Know

What is the dress code?

Black tie. We invite you to dress for an evening of grace and refinement: tuxedos and dark formal suits with black tie for gentlemen, and floor-length gowns or refined formal attire for ladies. Sundance maintains a strict black tie dress code, and we kindly ask that it be honored. Guests whose attire does not meet it will, with regret, be turned away. As the evening air in the mountains is cool, a wrap or topcoat is a lovely addition.

Are children welcome?

Children are warmly welcome at the reception. The ceremony, which takes place on the mountain, is reserved for adult guests.

How do we reach the ceremony?

By ski lift, up the mountain. Please dress in black tie, choose a wrap or topcoat for the cooler air, wear sensible closed-toe shoes, and plan to arrive at the base a little early. Guests with mobility concerns are welcome to reach out so we can make arrangements.

Why no phones, cameras or bags?

We would like everyone fully present for our vows, and the lift ride is easier without belongings. Phones, cameras, wallets and purses will not be permitted at the ceremony, and secure storage will be provided at the base. Our photographer will capture the day, and we will share every photo with you.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation will indicate who it’s addressed to. Please reach out if you have any questions.

What if the weather changes?

Mountain weather is unpredictable. We have a plan for every forecast, and we’ll keep everyone updated.

Is there a registry?

Your presence is the greatest gift. Registry details will be shared closer to the date.
